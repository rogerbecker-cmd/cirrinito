In short

Cirrinito is a 3D adventure game; its main character is called Lumo. The game works without an internet connection. The app does not collect any personal data: there is no user account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no cookies and no server of our own. We do not learn who plays the game.

2. Game progress and settings

The levels you have reached, your best times, your finds, your home among the clouds, your settings and which stories are unlocked are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us.

If backups are switched on for your device (iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad, Google backup on Android), your progress may be included in that backup. Apple and Google carry out these backups under their own privacy policies; we have no access to them.

In the menu, “New Game” deletes your progress (purchased stories are kept). Deleting the app deletes all data stored on the device.

3. In-app purchases

The first three levels and the open world Sparkle Bay are free. Each of the five stories can be bought with a one-time purchase through Apple’s App Store or Google Play. Payment and billing are handled exclusively by Apple or Google. We do not receive any payment details, names or email addresses.

So that purchased stories can be unlocked and restored on a new device, the app asks the store which content was bought with your store account. This information is only used on the device. For this the app uses Apple’s purchase interface (StoreKit) or Google’s “Google Play Billing Library”, integrated via the “Unity In-App Purchasing” library, which runs on the device and only connects to Apple or Google. The app needs an internet connection only for purchases and restoring them, not for playing.

Apple and Google process data about purchases and your store account as independent controllers. Their privacy policies apply: Apple and Google.

4. Age information

Where the law requires it (for example in some US states), the App Store may tell the app an age range (for example “13–15”), but never a date of birth. This information stays on the device and is only used to handle purchases and parental consent in line with the law.

5. Crash reports and statistics

The app contains no tools of its own for crash reports or usage statistics; the analytics and diagnostics services of the Unity game engine are switched off. Apple and Google provide app developers with aggregated statistics and crash reports if you have agreed to this in your device settings (for example “Share With App Developers” on iPhone and iPad). We receive this information without names or contact details and cannot link it to any person.

6. Permissions

Cirrinito does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or any other personal content on your device.

7. Contact by email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer your request and delete them once they are no longer needed for that purpose and no legal retention obligation applies. Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR (answering your request).

8. This website

We ourselves use no cookies, no scripts, no fonts from third-party servers and no analytics or tracking tools on these pages. 9. Children

Cirrinito is meant for adults and children aged 10 and over. The app contains no advertising, no chat and no external links and collects no personal data – including from children. Purchases are always confirmed by the App Store or Google Play (for children’s accounts with parental consent).

10. Your rights

Under the FADP and, where applicable, the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. As the app does not send any data to us, this usually only concerns emails you have sent us. Please contact the address above.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority – in Switzerland with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU with the authority of your country of residence.

11. Changes

We update this privacy policy when the app or the law changes. The version published here applies.
